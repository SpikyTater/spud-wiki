!title Cockatoo (and Poop)
!contributor BlueStrategosJ
!redirect Poop

Pearl is a big fan of her home country of Australia and decided to model much of her S11 base after Australia. Naturally, this included its native wildlife, with Pearl wanting to create trees full of **cockatoos** and cockatiels. For this purpose, she started collecting grey parrots on stream (as they look the closest to cockatoos/cockatiels). Currently, she plans to find a grey parrot in every stream to fill out her trees. {1}

To increase the realism of her cockatoo trees, Pearl has insisted on adding feathers, seeds and, most importantly, bird **poop** on the floor under the trees. To get bird poop into Minecraft, Pearl requested the creation of poop from fellow hermit ZombieCleo.{2} After a humorous conversation with Cleo (which included far too many mentions of poop), Cleo promised to make some poop for Pearl and delivered it within the same stream. On the question of payment, Cleo refused any compensation with the words, "What Pearl wants. Pearl gets." {3} After a continued discussion where Pearl and Cleo argued about who the poop truly belongs to (User vs. Creator), Pearl started distributing the goods under her cockatoo trees. Overall, the Twitch and YT audiences loved both Pearl's and Cleo's perspectives of the poop talk and will certainly not let either of them forget it. {4}

Like the previous year, Pearl wants to paint a picture to auction off at Gamer's Outreach 2026. This time Pearl decided on painting a pink cockatoo, certainly inspired by her recent cockatoo saga. While Pearl's artistry is without doubt and her sketches of the cockatoo are already wonderful, her focus on poop in HC has inspired her chat, who insisted on the inclusion of bird poop in the painting to Pearl's continued chagrin. On the chat comment that she started the whole poop conversation, she insisted that she is also ending it, which her chat doesn't believe. {5}

Another funny incident happened during the painting of the cockatoo. To quickly inform new chatters, Pearl's mods pinned a message of "Pearl is painting a pink cockatoo." But depending on the wideness of one's screen, the message could cut off at an unfortunate place, resulting in "Pearl is painting a pink cock..." Several chatters discovered this to their amusement, and the mods had to edit the message. As far as everybody knows, Pearl remains blissfully unaware of this.
